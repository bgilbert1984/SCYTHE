# GraphOps Causal-Hologram Directives

These directives treat the globe as a hypothesis compiler rather than a
conventional dashboard. Every execution must distinguish observed, measured,
solver-derived, inferred, synthetic, and counterfactual information.

## Signature directive

> Determine what must be true for this red coverage gap and this network burst
> to share a cause.

Construct competing causal worlds, identify the assumptions required by each
world, show supporting and contradicting evidence, and propose the least
expensive observation that would distinguish them.

## Explain a visible condition

- Explain why this cell is red. Trace its threshold decision, path-loss
  value, interpolation, source tile, contract lineage, transmitter geometry,
  and neighboring-field gradient.
- Explain why this region changed color.** Compare current and previous field
  states and separate new evidence from threshold, camera, configuration, and
  rendering changes.
- Identify the weakest assumption behind this overlay.** Rank assumptions by
  how much the conclusion changes when each is removed.
- Isolate the measured portions of this picture.** Dim solver output,
  inference, synthetic content, and illustration.
- Construct falsifiers for this conclusion.** Specify the observations needed
  to test each falsifier.
- Reveal the authoritative value beneath this display value.** Follow the
  derived visualization back to its immutable dataset asset without promoting
  the display tile to authority.

## Compare competing worlds

- Construct the strongest benign explanation.** Evaluate terrain, equipment,
  clock, data-quality, and configuration causes before escalating to an
  adversarial hypothesis.
- Construct the adversarial explanation with the fewest assumptions.** Find
  the minimum causal chain that could produce the evidence and label it as a
  hypothesis.
- Expose disagreements among the leading explanations.** Render only the
  cells, entities, edges, and predicted observations that differ among worlds.
- Extract the facts shared by all plausible worlds.** Preserve only invariants
  that survive every viable explanation.
- Rank worlds by explanatory economy.** Score evidence coverage,
  contradictions, assumptions, source independence, and stale evidence rather
  than model confidence alone.
- Separate the benign and adversarial worlds.** Display them side by side
  with synchronized cameras and a causal-difference overlay.

## Execute counterfactuals

- Move this transmitter here and recompute coverage.** Run a sandboxed
  propagation scenario and retain its results in a counterfactual world.
- Infer transmitter regions that could explain this field.** Return candidate
  regions instead of a fabricated point estimate and visualize geometric
  ambiguity.
- Evaluate a directional antenna hypothesis.** Compare omnidirectional and
  candidate antenna-pattern worlds.
- Reclassify coverage at a 135 dB threshold.** Preserve the underlying solver
  values and change only the classification layer.
- Remove this sensor and recompute support.** Expose conclusions that depend
  on a single source.
- Shift network events by five seconds and repeat correlation.** Measure
  sensitivity to clock error and coincidence-window selection.
- Treat this emitter as benign and expose the residual.** Subtract the
  selected cause and display what remains unexplained.

## Navigate space and time

- Replay the first moment these worlds diverged.** Locate and animate the
  earliest evidence transition separating their causal histories.
- Render the field's memory scars.** Display persistent, repeated, and slowly
  decaying RF structures across prior windows.
- Reveal events immediately preceding this burst.** Query a bounded temporal
  neighborhood across RF, network, sensor, and operator sources.
- Trace this anomaly backward to its earliest supporting evidence.** Traverse
  provenance and temporal edges without replacing missing history with prose.
- Project where uncertainty will grow.** Model data staleness and sensor
  coverage loss, not physical events, unless a validated predictive model is
  available.
- Compare this hour with the same hour yesterday.** Normalize for sensor
  availability and configuration changes before reporting behavioral drift.

## Correlate RF and graph behavior

- **Find graph entities activated during this RF transition.** Run a
  time-bounded `RF_CORRELATE` query and report the null expectation for chance
  coincidence.
- **Search for network behavior repeatedly preceded by this emitter.** Separate
  recurrence, correlation, and causal evidence.
- **Rank network bursts by temporal support from RF evidence.** Use measured
  timestamps, uncertainty windows, and source independence.
- **Distinguish infrastructure rotation from multiple similar actors.** Apply
  identity stitching and embedding similarity while displaying conflicting
  identifiers.
- **Find synchronized activity without a shared network path.** Search for
  coordination visible in time or RF space but absent from ordinary graph
  topology.
- **Design the RF observation that most reduces uncertainty about this
  cluster.** Recommend frequency, location, duration, and required evidence
  quality.

## Interrogate evidence and provenance

- **Reveal who or what asserted this edge.** Identify the originating sensor,
  solver, model, operator, import, or transformation.
- **Map every claim that depends on this dataset.** Compute downstream
  provenance impact before replacement or invalidation.
- **Expose contradictions instead of consensus.** Retain mutually incompatible
  claims until adjudication.
- **Identify conclusions using stale evidence.** Compare claim time semantics
  with source freshness and the requested analysis window.
- **Expose every inference made from absence.** Verify that the relevant sensor
  was capable, active, correctly positioned, and temporally aligned.
- **Test whether quantization or interpolation explains this anomaly.** Compare
  the display transform with authoritative values and neighboring raw samples.
- **Find evidence supported by only one independent source.** Collapse apparent
  corroboration that shares a common upstream origin.

## Direct investigation through gestures

- **Circle a region.** Create a geofenced `FOCUS` scope and summarize evidence
  classes before offering hypotheses.
- **Draw a path.** Trace spatial, network, and temporal relationships along the
  corridor.
- **Connect an RF cell to a graph node.** Compile an `RF_CORRELATE` plan and
  reveal the generated DSL before execution.
- **Pin two moments on the timeline.** Generate a `GRAPH_DELTA` comparison.
- **Drag a transmitter.** Create a counterfactual world and rerun only affected
  propagation products.
- **Pull two world layers apart.** Reveal causal and evidentiary differences.
- **Long-press an assertion.** Open its Reality Prism: origin, authority,
  confidence, contradictions, freshness, and falsifier.

## Design observations

- **Place one additional receiver for maximum information gain.** Optimize
  across leading hypotheses subject to operator-provided constraints.
- **Calculate the shortest useful capture.** Estimate the minimum duration
  needed to distinguish predicted temporal signatures.
- **Select the next frequency to sample.** Rank frequencies by disagreement
  among worlds rather than visual salience.
- **List evidence obtainable without changing the environment.** Prefer passive
  and read-only observations.
- **Design the cheapest test that could falsify the leading hypothesis.**
  Return a bounded experiment and expected outcomes for every viable world.
- **State what must remain unknown after this test.** Declare experimental
  limits before collection begins.

## Govern Copilot and Autopilot

- **Explain this Autopilot escalation.** Show tier thresholds, detector inputs,
  evidence references, cooldowns, and deduplication decisions.
- **Re-run this card without model interpretation.** Produce the deterministic
  DSL and rule-based result for comparison.
- **Direct the Skeptic to attack this card.** Generate benign explanations,
  counterexamples, missing evidence, and a discriminating test.
- **Expose model disagreement.** Compare outputs without converting agreement
  into evidence.
- **Reveal Dream Mode activity.** List counterfactual experiments and questions
  without promoting them to observations.
- **Explain the absence of an alert.** Identify discarded, observation-only,
  cooldown, deduplicated, or insufficient-evidence outcomes.
- **Declare prohibited actions.** State the authority boundary and the human
  decision or additional evidence required to cross it.

## Control the world stack

- **Create a shadow world from this hypothesis.** Copy references rather than
  evidence and keep proposed causes isolated from the observed world.
- **Freeze the observed world.** Continue ingesting into a new time branch while
  preserving the selected evidentiary state for inspection.
- **Merge only corroborated claims into the reconstructed world.** Reject claims
  with unresolved contradictions or missing provenance.
- **Discard this shadow world.** Remove its counterfactual products without
  altering observations or authoritative assets.
- **Promote this claim for human review.** Package evidence, contradictions,
  assumptions, falsifiers, and authority requirements into an adjudication
  card.
- **Compare the current world stack with a prior investigation.** Match causal
  structure and evidence posture without importing stale conclusions.

## Directive execution contract

Every directive must return, where applicable:

1. The requested result, transformation, or world comparison.
2. Execution status: completed, partially completed, refused, or unavailable.
3. Evidence class and authority for every material claim.
4. Supporting and contradicting evidence references.
5. Assumptions and sensitivity to those assumptions.
6. Temporal scope and source freshness.
7. A falsifier or discriminating observation.
8. The generated GraphOps DSL or solver job specification.
9. Every state mutation performed or proposed.
10. A clear boundary between analysis, simulation, and authorized action.

Directives that mutate evidence, promote claims, initiate collection, or affect
external systems require the applicable authority gate. No directive may
promote a browser visualization, model agreement, or counterfactual result into
measured or authoritative evidence.
