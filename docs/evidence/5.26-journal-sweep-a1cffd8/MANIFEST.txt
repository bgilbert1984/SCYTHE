Known harness limitations, unrepaired:
  TIMEOUT = 1800 in sweep.py is absolute wall-clock, not relative to the host's
  measured baseline. A control row was observed at 979.6s on this machine, so a
  host ~2x slower can time out on a HEALTHY control and record n=0, which reads
  as a zero. Relativise before trusting a TIMED_OUT row from slower hardware.

  require_sane_baseline gates on count >= minimum, a FLOOR. A stale bundle with
  a lower expected count therefore launches against a newer tree without
  complaint. Verify the count equals the figure above rather than relying on the
  gate.
