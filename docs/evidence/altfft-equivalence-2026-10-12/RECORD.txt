METHOD (altfft_run.py)
  Only numpy.fft.fft is rebound, inside the experiment process, to a call-counting wrapper that
  forwards to the chosen backend. The package runner is executed unchanged via runpy. The runner
  still prints/records fft_backend as numpy.fft; that field in the result files is NOT evidence of
  the backend used. Thresholds and the 1e-9 tolerance are unchanged. fftshift and fftfreq were
  not substituted.

CORRECTION TO A SIDECAR FIELD
  The scipy sidecar contains "backends_active": "1". That value is scipy.fft.get_workers()
  (a worker-count observation), mislabelled by the experiment script. It is NOT a backend identity.
  The sidecar is preserved byte-for-byte; the executed backend is identified by the recorded
  "module" and "name" fields above and by the call counts.
