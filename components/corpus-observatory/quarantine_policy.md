# Quarantine policy

Every imported corpus is provenance **EXTERNAL**, admission **INADMISSIBLE**.

- External data is a test vector or content. It is never promoted to measurement evidence, and never
  enters the §5.21 catalogue.
- The marks are set at import and are not editable by a later step, including a favourable benchmark.
- Reports about a corpus are SCYTHE's own observations of that corpus, with SCYTHE's provenance; they
  do not change the corpus's status.
- An imported corpus must not feed anything that reads as evidence about a live site, receiver or
  emitter.
- Licence obligations travel with the record (for example CC-BY-4.0 attribution before anything is
  published).

Quarantine is the existing posture for Hugging Face data in SCYTHE, restated here so the Observatory
inherits it instead of re-deciding it per corpus.
